## Ebook form – receive leads in Gmail

The Marathi AI guide form is connected to **Formspree** (form ID: `mqedokpv`). When someone enters name, email, and phone and gets the download link, you receive their details at the email set in your Formspree account (e.g. bitworldtechnologies@gmail.com).

To change the destination email or form, edit the form’s `action` URL in **index.html** or create a new form at [formspree.io](https://formspree.io) and replace the form ID in the URL. Formspree free tier allows 50 submissions per month.
